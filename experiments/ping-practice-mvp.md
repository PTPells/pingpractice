# Ping Practice MVP

{% hint style="info" %}
This experiment started in June 2024 and is ongoing
{% endhint %}

By summer 2024, at least two things were true of Ping Practice:

1. A team of talented people had aligned around developing an MVP that embodied Ping Practice in form and feel
2. The requirement for the MVP had become clear through making six prototypes over the previous three years

## Research questions <a href="#description" id="description"></a>

Can we build an experience that causes the following?

1. _Inspires people to revisit in ways that help them discover new meaning_
2. _Relieves people of the pull to knowing what a Ping might mean, and how/if they might use it_

# Evergreen

### Ping Practitioners – who we are

Ping Practice feels natural for people who already maintain a journaling practice or did at some point previously.&#x20;

We are people who tend to, "live in our heads" and deeply feel how overwhelming and inspiring this can be.

We have experienced the relief and clarity that can come from slowing down down and writing down the thoughts and feelings that move us.

We feel energized by connecting with others who resonate with our experience and feel moved to make meaning/learn from what we experience and offer that as a means to discover people who might be similar.

We experience an accute need for Ping Practice in moments of transition and uncertainty. Be it moving to a new place, being out of routine, leaving a relationship, etc.
